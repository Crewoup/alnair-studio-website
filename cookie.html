Cookie Policy

Last Updated: 2026-03-26


1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website recognize your device and remember information about your visit (like your language preference or login status).


2. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: These are essential for the website to function properly (e.g., account login, security). They cannot be switched off.
  • Analytical/Performance Cookies: We use services like Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site, helping us improve our services. All data is aggregated and anonymous.
  • Functional Cookies: These remember choices you make (like your username) to provide a more personalized experience.
  • Targeting/Marketing Cookies: (Optional) Used to deliver advertisements more relevant to you and your interests.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed by third-party services that appear on our pages, such as Google Cloud (for CaptainAI's AI processing) or external social media links. We do not control these third-party cookies.


4. How to Manage Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by setting your preferences in the following ways:


A. Browser Controls

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. To find out more about how to manage and delete cookies, visit:

  • AllAboutCookies.org
  • AboutCookies.org

B. Opting Out of Analytics

To specifically prevent your data from being used by Google Analytics across all websites, you can install the official opt-out tool provided by Google:

  • Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

C. Withdrawing Consent (Singapore PDPA Compliance)

If you are using our services and wish to withdraw your consent for non-essential cookies previously accepted, you may clear your browser’s cache and cookies, then refresh the page to reset your preferences via our cookie banner.


5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies, please contact us at service@alnair-studio.com.
